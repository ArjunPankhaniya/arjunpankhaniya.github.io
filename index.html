One Patient, One
Smooth Flow

Small hospitals usually juggle paper slips between reception, the doctor and the X-ray room. I built a simple system where a patient is registered once, gets a token, sees the doctor, goes for tests and comes back with a single printed summary, all on the hospital's own network.

  • Reception registers the patient, picks the doctor, charges new or old case fee and assigns an OPD room.
  • Doctor sees history and past reports, writes remarks, orders tests and prescribes medicines.
  • Test queue keeps the patient waiting while the doctor moves on to the next token.
  • Reception again adds final charges and prints the case summary with prescription and reports.
Note: The hospital name is withheld until the deployment is finalised. The demo on the right uses dummy data only and no real patient information.
