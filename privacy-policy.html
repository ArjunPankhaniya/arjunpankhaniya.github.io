Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arjun Pankhaniya ("Developer", "I", "we", "us"), an individual developer based in Jamnagar, Gujarat, India, handles information in the mobile application Kishan Funding and any other app I publish on Google Play that links to this page (together, the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

The App does not require you to create an account, and I do not collect your name, phone number, email address, location or contact list. Calculator inputs stay on your device.

2. Information the App does not collect

  • No registration, login or user profile is required.
  • The App does not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • The App does not show third-party advertising and does not sell or rent personal data.

3. Information processed on your device

Values you enter in the calculators (for example land area, crop, loan amount, turnover, interest rate and tenure), your language choice and any saved settings are processed and stored locally on your device only. I do not receive or have access to this data. Uninstalling the App removes it.

4. Internet use and live APMC prices

The core calculators work offline. When you open the live APMC mandi price feature, the App requests public market-price data from an online data source. Like any internet request, this may expose technical information such as your IP address, device/browser type and request time to that server. I do not link this information to you, and the App does not send your calculator inputs with the request.

5. PDF reports and sharing

PDF reports are generated on your device. When you choose to share one (for example via WhatsApp or Email), it is handed to the app you select through Android's share function. That app's own privacy policy applies to the file once shared. I do not receive a copy.

6. Permissions

The App requests only the permissions needed for its features, such as internet access for live prices and, where required by your Android version, access to save or share generated PDF files. You can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

7. Third-party services

The App does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics services. Google Play and your device operating system may collect their own data under Google's policies (policies.google.com/privacy). If a future version adds any third-party service (for example crash reporting), this policy will be updated before release.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because data stays on your device, you control it. You can delete it by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the App. If you have contacted me by email, I keep that correspondence only as long as needed to respond, and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

9. Security

I use reasonable measures to protect the App, including keeping core functionality on-device and using secure (HTTPS) connections where the App goes online. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended for bank officials, field officers, farmers and adults working with agricultural finance. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Financial disclaimer

Calculations are tentative estimates for informational purposes. Final loan approvals are subject to individual bank policies. See the Terms & Conditions for details.

12. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
